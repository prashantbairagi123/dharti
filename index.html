Step 1

Know your footprint

Eight quick answers. Dharti estimates your yearly carbon footprint using Indian emission factors and shows where it comes from.

Step 2

Pick your LiFE swaps

28 everyday swaps across the seven Mission LiFE themes. Tap the ones you'll do. Each adds a petal to your rangoli and adds up your yearly savings.

Indicative yearly savings per person or household, at ₹7/kWh and ₹100/L petrol. Your numbers will vary.

Step 3

Now multiply it

One person's swaps look small. Slide to see what happens when your family, your city or all of India does the same.

Step 4

The 21-day LiFE challenge

Habits take about three weeks to stick. One small action a day. Tap a day when you've done it and grow from a seed into a banyan.

Step 5

Before you buy

The greenest product is often the one you don't buy. Five honest questions, one clear answer.

Step 6

Become a Pro-Planet Person

Add your name to make a pledge card with your swaps, your savings and your rangoli. Share it to invite others.
