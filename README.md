# Dharti – Small swaps. Planet-sized impact.

**Live website:** https://prashantbairagi123.github.io/dharti/

Dharti (धरती, *Earth*) is a colourful, interactive website that turns India's **Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment)** into something anyone can play with. Measure your footprint, pick everyday swaps, and watch your personal rangoli bloom as your impact grows.

## Six interactive tools

| Step | Tool | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Know your footprint** | Eight quick questions give your yearly CO₂e using Indian emission factors. It compares you with India, the world and the 1.5°C target. |
| 2 | **LiFE swaps** | 28 swaps across the 7 Mission LiFE themes, with CO₂, ₹, water and plastic saved. |
| 3 | **Multiply** | Scales your swaps from you to all of India: trees, cars and Olympic pools. |
| 4 | **21-day challenge** | One action a day. Grow from Seed to Banyan. |
| 5 | **Before you buy** | Five honest questions tell you to skip it, borrow it, buy second-hand, or buy well. |
| 6 | **Pledge card** | A downloadable Pro-Planet Person card with your swaps and rangoli. |

## Highlights
- **Living rangoli:** every swap adds coloured petals, one ring for each LiFE theme.
- **Works offline** after the first visit, and installs on phones with "Add to Home screen".
- **Lightweight:** one ~155 KB file with fonts built in, no libraries and no tracking.
- **Private:** choices stay in your browser.

## Files
`index.html` (the whole site) · `sw.js`, `manifest.json`, `icon-*.png` (offline install)

## Honest limits
Footprints and savings are indicative estimates from published average factors, not measurements.
